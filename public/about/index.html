How I work

I’ve always loved helping people find a way forward.

In my insurance career, that meant helping people navigate an industry that could feel confusing and frustrating. I loved making the complicated understandable, finding solutions, and making sure people felt they had someone in their corner. I bring that same heart for service to real estate.

Buying or selling a home is about more than a property. It can mean a fresh start, a difficult goodbye, more room to grow, or the chance to build a different life. My goal is to understand what matters to you, help you see your options, and guide you through the steps with care, honesty, and practical support.

Why we moved

My own fresh start brought me to Tennessee.

I was born in Chicago, and that city will always have a piece of my heart—along with the Chicago Bears. But I grew up in California and have always described myself as a California girl with a Midwestern heart.

Over time, my husband and I began asking whether the future we imagined for our family was still the future we could build where we were. We wanted more room to grow, a life aligned with our values, and greater financial breathing room. Above all, we wanted an environment where we and our daughter could thrive.

We fell in love with Tennessee’s greenery, natural beauty, welcoming people, and sense of space. We loved the opportunity to explore a whole new part of the country, with so many places within driving distance. What began as imagining a different life became the bold decision to move across the country and build one.

“I didn’t move here to change Tennessee. I moved here because I love Tennessee.”
What I bring

That experience shapes how I want to help others.

I understand that relocation can be exciting and intimidating at the same time. There are practical questions to answer, emotional decisions to work through, and moments when you need someone to help connect the dots.

Whether you’re relocating, buying your first home, or preparing to sell, I bring a problem-solving mindset and an eye for possibility. I enjoy seeing both what a property offers today and what it could become—helping buyers recognize potential and sellers bring their home’s strengths into focus.
