Interested in a location that isn’t listed? Ask me. I may still be able to help — and if not, I’ll connect you with a reputable referral.

Can you help me sell in California and buy in Tennessee?

Yes. I’m licensed in both states, so I can help on both ends of your move — coordinating the timing of your sale and your purchase so you’re not juggling two agents and two separate plans.

What if I’m not moving from California?

No problem at all! If you have a home to sell in another state, I have a wonderful group of referral partners who can help with that sale. Relocation is relocation — no matter which state you’re moving from, the steps are much the same. I know firsthand what it takes to turn the dream of a new beginning into reality, and I hope you’ll choose Tennessee, just as I did.

Why Compass in Tennessee and Coldwell Banker in California?

Compass and Coldwell Banker are now part of the same company, Compass International Holdings. In Tennessee, I felt the best way to serve my clients was with Compass — a fantastic company with amazing resources, early access to many homes before they reach the wider market, and incredible tools that help your transaction run smoothly.

In California, I work with Coldwell Banker. Because the two share many of the same tools, I can bring those same resources to my clients in either state.

Why does your insurance background matter?

Insurance is part of what a home really costs to own. Because I owned an insurance agency for 10+ years, I look at things like roof age, flood and storm exposure, and construction costs early — so they don’t become a surprise late in the process.

Unfortunately, in today’s market, insurance is getting in the way of many deals closing — or adding to a buyer’s worries. I know how to navigate those frustrations, even when they come up at the last moment. No matter the state, I have resources and reputable professionals ready to help us work through any insurance issue that arises.

Do you work with real estate investors?

Yes. In Tennessee, I help investors look at single-family and small multifamily properties — requesting rent rolls and expenses, and laying out what’s known and what still needs checking before you commit.

In California, my mom and I have a lot of experience working with experienced investors, especially in the Bay Area, where we help locate properties to purchase, renovate, and resell.
